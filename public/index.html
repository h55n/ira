How it works

She checks the book, then she books.

Every slot Ira offers comes from your live book, so nothing gets made up. A 60-minute facial blocks both half-hour cells.

1

Picks up on the first ring

Streaming speech-to-text tuned for phone cadence, and callers can interrupt her.

2

Checks, offers, books

3

Why Ira

Built for the way local India books.

1st ring

Missed calls are missed bookings.

When you are mid-haircut or with a patient, the phone keeps ringing. Ira picks up and turns the call into a confirmed appointment.

WhatsApp confirmations

Sent through the Meta Cloud API. In demo mode the full message trail is logged.

Barge-in turn taking

Callers can talk over her. She stops, listens, and carries on like a person would.

Grounded, never inventive

Only books slots the engine returned. Only quotes catalog prices. Always confirms name and phone first.

Reschedule and cancel

By phone number, saved to disk.

Owner trail

Every booking and message is logged for the owner.

Swap the brain

Any OpenAI-compatible model, set by env.
