How it works

Call aya, booking ho gayi.

Three things happen in the time it used to take to find a pen.

1

Customer calls

Ira picks up on the first ring - Hindi, English, ya dono mix. She greets them like your best front-desk person would.

2

Ira checks the calendar

A 60-minute facial blocks both half-hour cells. Ira checks real availability and offers the nearest open slot if their pick is taken.

3

Confirmed on WhatsApp

Booking ID, date, time - straight to the customer's WhatsApp. Reschedule or cancel later with one call, same number.

Under the hood
